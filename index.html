Step-by-step calculation

1
Per Packet Weight (grams) (L × W × GSM × Sheets per packet) / 10000

Where L = length in cm, W = width in cm, GSM = grams per sq. meter per ply, Sheets = sheets in the packet (100, 85, 75, 70, 60, or 50).

2
Packets per kg 1000 / Per Packet Weight
3
Paper cost per packet Raw Material Rate (₹/kg) / Packets per kg
4
Total cost per packet Paper cost + Other expenses
5
Wholesale price Total cost + ₹2   ·   Retail price Total cost + ₹4
Shape rule of thumb: square (L = W) is the best tissue shape. Near-square (|L − W| ≤ 10% of the smaller side, i.e. ratio up to ~1.10:1) is also good. Anything more rectangular than that isn't a standard tissue shape and should be reviewed.
